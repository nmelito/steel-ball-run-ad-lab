6,000 Kilometres. 50,000,000 Dollars.

On September 25th, riders from across the world will depart San Diego Beach in pursuit of the grand prize. The route crosses the Arizona Desert, the Rocky Mountains, the Great Plains, and finishes in New York City. Any horse, any rider, any method — so long as you reach each checkpoint.
